MedEssay Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026 | Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MedEssay. MedEssay is a web-based study tool designed to help preclinical medical students practise essay-style questions and improve their academic writing. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of our platform at medessay.netlify.app. By using MedEssay, you agree to the terms described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the platform.

2. Who Uses MedEssay

MedEssay is intended for university students, primarily those in preclinical medical and health sciences programmes. Our users are generally 16 years of age and older. We do not specifically target children under the age of 13. If you are under 13, please do not use MedEssay without verifiable parental or guardian consent. If we become aware that a user under 13 has provided us with personal data without such consent, we will take steps to delete that information.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Currently

MedEssay is currently in beta and does not require account creation or collect personal data during normal use. You can access the platform's study features without providing any personal information.

3.2 In Future Versions

As MedEssay grows, we may collect the following types of information: Contact information (e.g. name, email address) if you sign up for an account or subscribe to updates Usage data (e.g. which topics you study, time spent on the platform) to improve the learning experience Feedback and responses you voluntarily submit through forms or surveys Device and browser information for technical diagnostics and performance optimisation We will update this Policy before any new data collection begins and will request your consent where required by law.

4. Beta Testing Programme

If you apply to be a MedEssay beta tester, you will be asked to provide your name, email address, institution, and other voluntary information through a Google Form. This data is used solely to:

  • Evaluate your suitability as a beta tester
  • Contact you about participation and feedback
  • Improve MedEssay based on your responses

Beta tester data is stored in Google Workspace (Google Forms/Sheets) and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy in addition to this one. We will not share your beta application data with third parties. You may withdraw from the programme at any time by contacting us.

5. How We Use Your Information

Any information collected is used to:

  • Operate and improve the MedEssay platform
  • Respond to enquiries and support requests
  • Understand how students use the tool so we can enhance learning outcomes
  • Send product updates or important notices (only where you have opted in)

We will never use your data for unrelated commercial purposes or sell it to third parties.

6. Cookies and Tracking

MedEssay may use basic cookies or browser storage to maintain your session or remember your preferences within a visit. We do not currently use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking technologies. As the platform develops, we will introduce a cookie notice if more tracking tools are added.

7. Third-Party Services

MedEssay is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may collect standard server logs (IP addresses, browser type, page requests) as part of normal hosting operations. Please refer to Netlify’s Privacy Policy for details. We may also use:

  • Google Forms — for beta tester applications and feedback collection
  • FormSubmit — for contact form submissions

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy, and we encourage you to review them.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect any data we hold. However, no internet transmission is completely secure. We recommend that you do not submit highly sensitive personal information through the platform.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as MedEssay evolves. When we do, we will revise the “Last Updated” date at the top of this document. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.
